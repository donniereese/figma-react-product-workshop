# Interview Presentation Outline (10–15 minutes)

1. **Context and challenge**
   - Business domain
   - Problem stakes
   - Success criteria

2. **Discovery and framing**
   - Roles and workflow findings
   - Key constraints
   - Scope rationale

3. **Design progression**
   - IA and task prioritization
   - Lo-fi flow decisions
   - Hi-fi prototype highlights

4. **System and accessibility choices**
   - Component strategy
   - State coverage
   - Accessibility expectations

5. **React handoff readiness**
   - Component mapping and behavior notes
   - Responsive and interaction requirements
   - Implementation constraints and open questions

6. **Outcome and reflection**
   - Expected impact and KPIs
   - Tradeoffs made
   - Next validation/build steps
