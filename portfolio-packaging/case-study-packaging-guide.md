# Case Study Packaging Guide

## Present the problem clearly
- Describe business stakes and affected workflows.
- Explain who was impacted and what was not working.
- Define target outcomes in business and user terms.

## Explain your process
- Show framing, role mapping, IA, flow design, and hi-fi progression.
- Use artifacts as evidence of decisions.
- Highlight where assumptions were validated or revised.

## Justify design decisions
- Tie each major decision to workflow efficiency, risk reduction, or clarity.
- Explain why alternatives were deprioritized.
- Show how design system and accessibility choices supported scale.

## Talk about tradeoffs
- Clarify constraints (legacy systems, compliance, deadlines).
- Explain speed vs depth decisions and what was deferred.
- Identify risks you intentionally accepted and why.

## Discuss React implementation readiness
- Show component boundaries and state model preparedness.
- Explain responsive behavior and interaction logic.
- Demonstrate what engineering received for efficient handoff.

## Package final artifacts
- 1 narrative deck or long-form writeup
- 1 concise artifact index with links/screens
- 1 implementation-readiness summary for design-engineering audiences
