# Tables, Filters, and Reporting Patterns

## Core principles
- Optimize for scan, compare, and act
- Keep filtering predictable and reversible
- Support decision logging and export expectations

## Reusable patterns
- Sticky headers with sortable critical columns
- Saved filter presets by role/use-case
- Side-panel detail inspection from row selection
- Bulk action bar with clear eligibility rules

## Implementation-aware notes
- Define server/client filtering assumptions early.
- Specify pagination, virtualization, and empty-result behavior.
