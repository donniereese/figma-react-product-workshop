# Module 01: Business Context and Product Framing

## Concept
Strong product design starts with the business problem, not the interface. In enterprise and healthcare contexts, unclear framing leads to expensive rework.

## Practical guidance
- Define business goals, constraints, and decision-makers.
- Identify where current workflows create cost, delay, or risk.
- Translate broad asks into a focused product opportunity statement.

## Key questions
- What is the business trying to improve (speed, quality, compliance, throughput)?
- Who signs off on success?
- What outcomes matter within 30–90 days?

## Artifact to create
- **Problem Framing One-Pager**: context, current-state pain points, target outcomes, assumptions, and risks.

## Common gotchas
- Starting UI before confirming business constraints.
- Defining success as “better UX” instead of measurable impact.
- Ignoring operational realities (approvals, policy, regulation).

## Interview/workshop prompts
- “How did you move from a broad request to a focused product scope?”
- “What tradeoffs did you make because of business constraints?”

## Key takeaways
- Business alignment is the foundation of credible design decisions.
- Clear framing improves both design quality and implementation speed.
