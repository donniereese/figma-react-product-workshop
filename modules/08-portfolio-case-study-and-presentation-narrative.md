# Module 08: Portfolio Case Study and Presentation Narrative

## Concept
A strong case study demonstrates product judgment, process rigor, and implementation awareness.

## Practical guidance
- Build a narrative from business problem to shipped-ready direction.
- Show decision checkpoints, tradeoffs, and constraint handling.
- Present outcomes through artifacts, not claims.

## Key questions
- What was the high-stakes business context?
- Which insights changed your direction?
- How did your decisions support both users and engineering delivery?

## Artifact to create
- **Case Study Deck/Writeup** with evidence-backed storyline and interview talking points.

## Common gotchas
- Showing final screens without process evidence.
- Overstating impact without measurable proxy outcomes.
- Failing to explain compromises and alternatives.

## Interview/workshop prompts
- “What would you test next before implementation?”
- “What tradeoffs did you make between speed, risk, and usability?”

## Key takeaways
- Portfolio strength comes from reasoning quality and artifact coherence.
- Implementation readiness is a differentiator for hybrid candidates.
