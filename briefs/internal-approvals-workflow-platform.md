# Brief: Internal Approvals and Workflow Platform

## Business context
A mid-size enterprise runs procurement, policy, and budget approvals through email and disconnected tools, creating long cycle times and weak accountability.

## Users and roles
- Requestor
- Team Manager
- Finance Approver
- Compliance Reviewer
- Workflow Administrator

## Problem statement
Approval requests lack standardized context, routing rules are inconsistent, and stakeholders cannot reliably track request status.

## Constraints
- Must support configurable approval chains
- Must preserve audit trail for decisions
- Needs phased migration from existing manual process

## Success metrics / KPIs
- Reduced median approval cycle time
- Increased first-pass approval completion rate
- Lower volume of “status check” messages
- Improved SLA compliance by request type

## Suggested deliverables
- Role and journey map for approvals lifecycle
- IA for queue, request details, and policy rules
- Wireframes and prototype for submit/review/approve/reject flow
- React handoff with status model and exception states

## Why this is portfolio-worthy
It showcases workflow UX, decision support, and governance-aware product design in a real enterprise operations scenario.
