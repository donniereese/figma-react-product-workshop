# Brief: Legacy Enterprise Tool Modernization

## Business context
A business-critical internal tool has grown over 10+ years, resulting in inconsistent UI, high training time, and frequent operator mistakes.

## Users and roles
- Daily Operations Specialist
- Senior Analyst
- Team Lead
- IT Support / Platform Owner

## Problem statement
The existing system is feature-rich but hard to navigate, with outdated patterns that slow experienced users and overwhelm new hires.

## Constraints
- No full rebuild; modernization must be incremental
- Back-end contracts remain mostly fixed in phase 1
- Must maintain parity for core workflows during transition

## Success metrics / KPIs
- Reduced onboarding time to task proficiency
- Lower critical task error rate
- Improved completion time for top recurring workflows
- Reduced support tickets tied to navigation confusion

## Suggested deliverables
- Current-state workflow and pain audit
- Prioritized modernization roadmap by workflow slice
- High-impact UI redesign prototype for selected flow
- Migration-ready handoff notes for React component mapping

## Why this is portfolio-worthy
It demonstrates systems thinking, phased transformation strategy, and practical modernization constraints common in enterprise environments.
